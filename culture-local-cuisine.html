Local Cuisine

Fresh seafood, volcanic-grown fruits, and time-honored recipes that reflect the warmth and richness of Camiguin's island culture.

A Taste of the Island

Camiguin's cuisine is a celebration of its natural abundance. The island's volcanic soil produces some of the finest tropical fruits in the Philippines, most notably the sweet lanzones. Coconut, banana, and jackfruit also figure prominently in the local diet.

Seafood is a cornerstone of Camiguin's culinary tradition. With the Bohol Sea on all sides, fresh fish, squid, shrimp, and crab are caught daily and prepared simply to let their natural flavors shine.

🍈 Camiguin Lanzones

Grown in the island's volcanic soil, Camiguin lanzones are renowned throughout the Philippines for being exceptionally sweet and seedless.

🐟 Fresh Seafood

From grilled tuna to kinilaw and sinigang, Camiguin's seafood dishes are fresh, flavorful, and affordable. Visit the local public market early in the morning for the freshest catch.

🥥 Pasalubong and Sweets

Take home uniquely Camiguin treats like pastillas de leche, dried lanzones, and coconut-based sweets sold at local markets and roadside stalls.
