Experiences

Adventure awaits on every shore — from pristine beaches to volcanic peaks and vibrant underwater worlds.

Beach & Swimming

Pristine shores, calm turquoise waters, and hidden coves await you along Camiguin's stunning volcanic coastline.

Camiguin's coastline is a swimmer's paradise. The island is ringed with clean, uncrowded beaches that offer calm and clear waters ideal for a relaxing dip or a full day of swimming. The volcanic geology of the island has created several unique swimming spots beyond the typical sandy beach.

Best Beaches

Yumbing Beach, Tangub Beach, and Agoho Beach are among the most popular and accessible beaches on the island.

Swimming Conditions

Waters are generally calm from November to June. Check local conditions before swimming.

Diving & Snorkeling

Explore vibrant coral gardens, the hauntingly beautiful Sunken Cemetery, and rich marine life in the Bohol Sea.

Camiguin's underwater environment is one of the most diverse and well-preserved in the Philippines. The waters surrounding the island are home to thriving coral reefs, abundant fish species, sea turtles, and unique dive sites—most notably the Sunken Cemetery.

Dive Sites

Sunken Cemetery, Mantigue Island, and coral gardens offer extraordinary underwater experiences.

Certifications

Several dive shops offer PADI certification courses and equipment rental for all skill levels.

Hiking & Trekking

Traverse volcanic slopes, explore lush rainforests, and discover hidden waterfalls on Camiguin's rewarding trail network.

Camiguin offers a remarkable range of hiking and trekking experiences, from gentle nature walks through coconut groves to the demanding summit trek of Mt. Hibok-Hibok. The most iconic trek is the ascent of Mt. Hibok-Hibok, an active stratovolcano that rewards climbers with panoramic 360-degree views.

Mt. Hibok-Hibok

1,332m summit trek takes 4-6 hours round-trip. Moderate to challenging difficulty.

Guides Available

Local guides are recommended, especially for first-time visitors. Start early for best conditions.

Island Hopping

Set sail and discover the hidden gems surrounding Camiguin — from White Island sandbars to secluded offshore reefs.

Island hopping is one of the most popular activities for visitors to Camiguin, offering a chance to explore diverse natural wonders by sea. A typical tour combines White Island, the Sunken Cemetery cross, coral garden swims, and Mantigue Island. Local boatmen offer flexible itineraries based on weather and your interests.

Tour Options

Half-day and full-day tours available. Boats depart from multiple jump-off points around the island.

Best Stops

White Island, Sunken Cemetery, coral gardens, and Mantigue Island are favorite tour destinations.
