Where to Stay in Camiguin

From cozy beachfront cottages to comfortable resorts, Camiguin offers welcoming accommodation options for every type of traveler and budget.

Accommodation on the Island

Camiguin's accommodation scene is intimate and charming. Most resorts and guesthouses are small, family-run establishments that offer personalized service. The majority of accommodations are concentrated along the northern coast near Mambajao.

Budget travelers will find clean and comfortable guesthouses at very reasonable prices. Mid-range resorts offer beachfront cottages and swimming pools. For those seeking more, several boutique resorts offer elevated amenities.

🏠 Where to Stay

Most accommodations are concentrated in Mambajao and Yumbing along the northern coast — the most convenient base for exploring White Island and Ardent Hot Springs.

💰 Budget Options

Guesthouses and transient rooms are available from very affordable nightly rates. Many include simple breakfast. Book in advance during peak season to secure availability.

🌴 Mid-Range and Boutique

Several beachfront resorts offer comfortable rooms, swimming pools, and on-site dining. Look for resorts in Yumbing or Agoho for the best combination of beach access and convenience.
