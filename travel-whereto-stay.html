Where to Stay in Camiguin

From cozy beachfront cottages to comfortable resorts, Camiguin offers welcoming accommodation options for every type of traveler and budget.

Accommodation on the Island

Camiguin's accommodation scene is intimate and charming, reflecting the island's unhurried and welcoming character. Most resorts and guesthouses are small, family-run establishments that offer personalized service and a genuine connection to the local community. The majority of accommodations are concentrated along the northern coast near Mambajao, with several options also found in Yumbing near White Island and in quieter areas around the island.

Budget travelers will find clean and comfortable guesthouses and transient rooms available at very reasonable prices. Mid-range resorts offer beachfront cottages, swimming pools, and restaurant facilities. For those seeking a more premium experience, several boutique resorts offer elevated amenities while maintaining the island's relaxed and authentic atmosphere.

Where to Stay

Most accommodations are concentrated in Mambajao and Yumbing along the northern coast — the most convenient base for exploring White Island, Ardent Hot Springs, and the town center.

Budget Options

Guesthouses and transient rooms are available from very affordable nightly rates. Many include simple breakfast. Book in advance during peak season (October and Holy Week) to secure availability.

Mid-Range and Boutique

Several beachfront resorts offer comfortable rooms, swimming pools, and on-site dining at mid-range prices. Look for resorts in Yumbing or Agoho for the best combination of beach access and convenience.
