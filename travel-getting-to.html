Getting to Camiguin

Whether you fly or sail, reaching Camiguin is the beginning of an unforgettable island adventure in the heart of the Philippines.

How to Reach the Island

Camiguin can be reached by air or sea, with the most common route being a combination of both. The nearest major airport is Cagayan de Oro (CDO), served by regular flights from Manila and Cebu. From CDO, travelers take a bus or van to Balingoan port, then a ferry to Camiguin.

The ferry crossing from Balingoan is the most reliable and scenic way to arrive, offering your first views of the island's dramatic volcanic peaks as you approach by sea.

✈ By Air

Fly to Cagayan de Oro (Laguindingan Airport) from Manila or Cebu via Cebu Pacific or Philippine Airlines. The airport is approximately 1.5 hours from the Balingoan ferry port.

⛴ By Ferry

Regular RORO ferries and speedboats operate between Balingoan (Misamis Oriental) and Benoni port in Camiguin. The journey takes approximately 1.5 hours for RORO and 45 minutes by speedboat.

🚌 From CDO to Balingoan

Buses and vans bound for Balingoan depart regularly from the Agora bus terminal in Cagayan de Oro. The trip takes approximately 2 hours.
