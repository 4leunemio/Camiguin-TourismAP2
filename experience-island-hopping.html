Island Hopping

Set sail and discover the hidden gems surrounding Camiguin — from the iconic White Island sandbar to secluded offshore reefs and volcanic islets.

Discover the Waters Around Camiguin

Island hopping is one of the most popular activities for visitors to Camiguin, offering a chance to explore the diverse natural wonders that surround the island by sea. A typical island hopping tour combines a visit to White Island, a snorkeling stop at the Sunken Cemetery cross, a swim at a coral garden, and a trip to the nearby Mantigue Island — a small, pristine island with powdery white sand and exceptional snorkeling.

Boats can be chartered from various jump-off points around the island, and local boatmen typically offer flexible itineraries based on weather conditions and your interests. Whether you want a full-day adventure or a quick afternoon excursion, the waters around Camiguin never disappoint.

Mantigue Island

A small protected marine sanctuary just off the coast of Mahinog. Its white sand beach, crystal-clear water, and thriving coral make it one of the most beautiful spots near Camiguin.

Booking a Boat

Bangka boats for island hopping can be hired from Yumbing Beach or the Mambajao port. Agree on the itinerary and price before departure. Half-day and full-day rates are available.

What to Bring

Pack sunscreen, a dry bag for electronics, snorkeling gear (or rent from your boatman), plenty of water, and snacks. Waterproof sandals are highly recommended.
