Island Hopping

Set sail and discover the hidden gems surrounding Camiguin — from the iconic White Island sandbar to secluded offshore reefs and volcanic islets.

Discover the Waters Around Camiguin

Island hopping is one of the most popular activities for visitors to Camiguin, offering a chance to explore the diverse natural wonders that surround the island by sea. A typical tour combines White Island, the Sunken Cemetery cross, a coral garden swim, and Mantigue Island.

Boats can be chartered from various jump-off points around the island, and local boatmen typically offer flexible itineraries based on weather conditions and your interests.

🏝 Mantigue Island

A small protected marine sanctuary just off the coast of Mahinog. Its white sand beach, crystal-clear water, and thriving coral make it one of the most beautiful spots near Camiguin.

⛵ Booking a Boat

Bangka boats for island hopping can be hired from Yumbing Beach or the Mambajao port. Agree on the itinerary and price before departure. Half-day and full-day rates are available.

💡 What to Bring

Pack sunscreen, a dry bag for electronics, snorkeling gear, plenty of water, and snacks. Waterproof sandals are highly recommended.
