Getting Around Camiguin

Camiguin is a small island that is easy to navigate — whether you prefer motorcycles, tricycles, or simply exploring on foot.

Navigating the Island

Getting around Camiguin is part of the adventure. The island has a single circumferential road that loops around its entire perimeter, making navigation straightforward and enjoyable. The total circuit is approximately 64 kilometers and can be completed by motorcycle in about 2–3 hours, making it one of the most popular ways to experience the island.

Public transport options include tricycles for short distances within municipalities and habal-habal (motorcycle taxis) for longer journeys between towns and attractions. Multicabs also operate between the main towns. For the most freedom and flexibility, renting a motorcycle or scooter is highly recommended and is available at various points around the island.

Motorcycle Rental

The most popular way to explore Camiguin. Scooters and motorcycles can be rented from several shops in Mambajao. Rates are very affordable. An international or Philippine driver's license is required.

Tricycles and Habal-Habal

Tricycles serve intra-municipality routes while habal-habal motorcycles connect towns and reach more remote attractions. Always agree on the fare before boarding.

Private Vehicle

Some resorts offer vehicle rentals with drivers for day tours. This is a comfortable option for groups or families who want a guided experience around the island's highlights.
