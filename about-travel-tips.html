Everything you need to know before you go — practical advice to help you make the most of your visit to Camiguin.

Practical Tips for Visiting Camiguin

Camiguin is a relatively easy destination to travel to and around, but a few practical tips will help ensure your visit goes smoothly. The island has a relaxed pace and limited commercial infrastructure compared to more developed tourist destinations — which is a big part of its charm, but also means it pays to come prepared.

Mobile signal is available across most of the island, with Globe and Smart networks providing the most reliable coverage. ATMs are available in Mambajao, but it is advisable to bring enough cash as machines can run out during peak periods. Credit cards are accepted at some larger resorts but rarely at smaller establishments, markets, and local eateries.

Money Matters

Bring sufficient cash as ATMs in Mambajao can occasionally be out of service. Small bills are useful for markets, tricycles, and local food stalls. Most small businesses do not accept cards.

Connectivity

Globe and Smart mobile networks provide good coverage across most of the island. Free WiFi is available at some resorts and cafes in Mambajao. Download offline maps before arriving as a backup.

Responsible Travel

Help preserve Camiguin's natural beauty by disposing of waste properly, avoiding damage to coral reefs, and supporting local businesses. The island's pristine environment depends on the mindfulness of its visitors.
