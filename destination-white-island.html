White Island

A pristine uninhabited sandbar rising from the turquoise waters of Camiguin, offering breathtaking views of Mt. Hibok-Hibok.

About White Island

White Island is one of the most iconic natural attractions in the Philippines. This uninhabited white sandbar sits just offshore from the municipality of Mambajao and is surrounded by the crystal-clear waters of the Bohol Sea. Unlike most islands, White Island has no permanent structures — just powdery white sand, endless sky, and an unobstructed panoramic view of Mt. Hibok-Hibok.

The sandbar shifts and changes shape with the seasons and tides. At low tide, it stretches wide and inviting; at high tide, portions disappear beneath the sea. Visitors can wade in the shallow waters, snorkel along the surrounding reef, or simply sit and absorb one of the most serene landscapes the Philippines has to offer.

🚤 How to Get There

Take a short 10–15 minute boat ride from the White Island jump-off point near Yumbing, Mambajao. Bangka boats are available for hire at the beach.

⏰ Best Time to Visit

Early morning offers the calmest waters and fewest crowds. The dry season from March to June provides the best visibility and weather conditions.

💡 Travel Tips

Bring sunscreen, water, and a hat — there is no shade on the sandbar. Snorkeling gear can be rented from boatmen. No food stalls are available on the island.
