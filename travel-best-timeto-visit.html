Best Time to Visit

Plan your trip around Camiguin's seasons to enjoy the best weather, clearest waters, and most vibrant cultural celebrations.

When to Go to Camiguin

Camiguin is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit is during the dry season, which runs from November to May. During these months, rainfall is minimal, skies are clear, and sea conditions are calm — ideal for island hopping, snorkeling, and beach activities. The peak of the dry season from March to May offers the warmest and most stable weather.

October is a particularly exciting time to visit thanks to the Lanzones Festival, the island's biggest annual celebration. Despite some rain during this shoulder season, the festive atmosphere more than compensates. The rainy season from June to September brings lush green scenery and fewer tourists, but rough seas can limit water activities.

Dry Season (November – May)

The dry season is widely regarded as the best time to visit the island, offering consistently sunny weather, calm seas, and ideal conditions for outdoor exploration. Travelers can enjoy uninterrupted island hopping, pristine beach days, and excellent visibility for snorkeling and diving. The clear waters provide optimal opportunities to experience the island’s rich marine life. This period also coincides with peak travel demand, particularly during major holidays such as Holy Week (March or April). Visitors should expect larger crowds and increased accommodation rates. To ensure a smooth and enjoyable trip, it is highly recommended to secure bookings in advance, including lodging and tours.

October — Festival Season

October brings a vibrant cultural experience to the island through the annual Lanzones Festival, a celebration that highlights local traditions and community spirit. Visitors can enjoy colorful street parades, energetic dance performances, and a variety of cultural events centered around the region’s famous lanzones fruit. Due to the popularity of the festival, the island experiences a surge in visitors during this time. Accommodation prices may rise, and availability becomes limited.

Early reservations are strongly advised, and guests should expect a more lively and festive atmosphere compared to quieter months.

Wet Season (June – September)

The wet season presents a quieter and more budget-friendly alternative for travelers seeking a more relaxed experience. During these months, the island becomes lush and vibrant, with greenery at its peak. Fewer crowds allow for a more peaceful stay, and accommodations are often more affordable compared to peak season.

However, weather conditions can be unpredictable, with occasional heavy rains and rougher seas. Some outdoor activities, such as trekking and boat excursions, may be limited. Despite these challenges, the island’s natural beauty remains captivating. Visitors are advised to bring rain gear and plan flexible itineraries.
